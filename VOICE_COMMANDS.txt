JARVIS QUICK COMMANDS

Tap the microphone and speak, or type a command:
- Open WhatsApp / WhatsApp kholo
- Open Instagram
- Open YouTube
- Open Facebook
- Open Chrome / browser kholo
- Open Google Maps
- Open Gmail
- Open Spotify
- Open Telegram
- Open Snapchat
- Open TikTok
- Open camera
- Open settings
- Open Wi-Fi settings
- Open Bluetooth settings
- Volume up / volume barhao
- Volume down / volume kam
- Search weather in Lahore
- Type Hello, how are you?

Optional wake phrase:
1. Open JARVIS and tap START HEY JARVIS LISTENING.
2. Keep the persistent listening notification visible.
3. Say: "Hey JARVIS, open WhatsApp".
4. Stop from the Stop action in the notification.

Optional text entry:
1. Tap ENABLE OPTIONAL PHONE CONTROL.
2. In Android Accessibility settings, enable JARVIS Phone Control.
3. Open the target app and tap its text box.
4. Return to JARVIS and say/type "type your message".
5. Review the entered text and manually press Send in the target app.

Privacy and safety:
- Wake phrase mode keeps the microphone service active until stopped.
- Android speech recognition may send audio to the phone's configured speech provider.
- Accessibility permission can inspect the active window to locate the focused text field.
- JARVIS never presses Send or silently sends a message.
- Do not enter passwords, payment details, or sensitive information through voice automation.
